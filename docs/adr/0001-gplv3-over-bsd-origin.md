# GPLv3 for the fork, over Dimension Warp's BSD-3-Clause origin

Dimension Warp is BSD-3-Clause (permissive); we license this fork under GPLv3 (copyleft) to match Multi-Team Support and, above all, to keep future adopters open — a permissive license would let a downstream fork close the source, the opposite of the goal. BSD-3 is GPL-compatible, so this is the canonical permissive→copyleft direction: the original BSD copyright and license text are retained in `LICENSE.dimension-warp` (we cannot and do not relicense Guillaume's code away from BSD), while the combined work and our additions are GPLv3.

Note for future readers: Factorio mods ship as readable Lua regardless of license, so the value GPL adds here is the *legal* guarantee that derivatives stay free — not mere source visibility.
